## Key Topics Covered

**Transformers & Attention Mechanisms:**
- Transformer architecture is the foundation for modern large language models
- Attention mechanisms allow models to focus on relevant information in input text

**Open-Source vs. Proprietary AI:**
- Open-source platforms (Hugging Face) allow customization and self-hosting
- Proprietary APIs (OpenAI) offer managed services without infrastructure management

**Privacy & Security Considerations:**
- Self-hosted solutions keep data on internal systems
- Cloud-based APIs require data transmission to external servers
- Organizations handling sensitive data (healthcare, finance) must consider compliance requirements

**Cost Analysis:**
- Self-hosted solutions involve upfront hardware and ongoing maintenance costs
- API-based services charge per usage without infrastructure overhead
